A little care goes a long way

Keeping Your Cake Perfect

For the best experience, follow these simple storage and serving recommendations.

❄

Keep Refrigerated

Store your cake in the refrigerator at 2–5°C.

◷

Freshness

Best enjoyed within 3 days. Once opened, consume within 24 hours.

♡

Before Serving

Allow your cake to rest for approximately 15 minutes at room temperature.

Keep upright and away from direct sunlight or excessive heat. Our cakes are made without preservatives.
