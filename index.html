A little care goes a long way

Keep it perfect.

A few simple tips to help you enjoy every last spoonful.

❄

Keep Chilled

Store in the refrigerator at 2–5 °C.

◷

Enjoy Fresh

Best enjoyed within 3 days of baking.

♧

Once Opened

Reseal and finish within 24 hours.

☀

Avoid Heat

Keep away from direct sunlight and hot cars.

♡

Before Enjoying

Rest for 15 minutes at room temperature.

↥

Carry Upright

Keep your can level when transporting.
