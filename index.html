    Storage & Serving Guide

    A little care keeps your cake delicious. Follow these recommendations for the best taste, texture and experience.

    ❄

    Keep Chilled

    Store in the refrigerator at 2–5°C. Keep upright and away from heat.

    ◷

    Once Opened

    Enjoy within 24 hours of opening. Keep covered and refrigerated.

    ♡

    Before Enjoying

    Remove from the refrigerator and allow approximately 20 minutes at room temperature before eating.

    Freshness tip: Enjoy within 3 days of purchase, provided the cake has been stored correctly and the use-by date on the packaging has not passed.

    How to Enjoy

    • Keep your cake chilled until you're ready.
    • Open carefully and enjoy the layers.
    • Allow approximately 20 minutes out of the refrigerator before eating.
    • Use a clean spoon every time.
    • Refrigerate promptly after serving.
